How We Drive Results

1

We build a content strategy designed to reach and engage decision-makers as they research, evaluate, and seek expertise — driving awareness, education, and warm inbound interest that feeds your pipeline.

What we deliver:
  • Audience research & ICP analysis
  • Competitive positioning review
  • Content strategy & messaging framework
  • Campaign timeline & success metrics
2

We identify influencers whose audiences actually match your ideal customer profile—not just follower counts. We analyze demographics, engagement quality, and audience authenticity to ensure you're reaching your buyers.

What we deliver:
  • Vetted influencer shortlist with audience breakdowns

  • Engagement & authenticity analysis
  • Partnership outreach & negotiation

We manage everything: influencer coordination, content planning, posting schedules, and approvals. We also set up tracking infrastructure (GA4, LinkedIn Insight Tag, UTM parameters) so you can measure every click, lead, and conversion.

What we deliver:
  • End-to-end campaign management
  • Content briefing, review & scheduling
  • Analytics setup & conversion tracking
  • Real-time performance monitoring
4

We go beyond vanity metrics. You'll get detailed reporting on ICP engagement, content effectiveness, website traffic, and lead generation—plus a list of high-value accounts showing intent and recommendations for sales follow-up.

What we deliver:
  • ICP-aligned engagement analysis
  • Content performance & cost efficiency (CPM, CPC, CPL)
  • Top engaged accounts & ABM opportunities
  • Monthly reports with strategic recommendations

Why B2B Creator Marketing Works

Backed by data from leading research firms

McKinsey Global B2B Pulse, 2024

10+ channels per buying journey

B2B buyers bounce across more than ten different channels before making a purchase. To reach them effectively, your brand needs trusted voices meeting them wherever they're actively researching, especially on LinkedIn.

Source: McKinsey & Company, "Five fundamental truths: How B2B winners keep growing," 2024.
LinkedIn B2B Marketing Benchmark, 2025

93.7% say trust is the #1 success factor

LinkedIn surveyed 1,500 B2B marketing leaders and asked what matters most for brand success. Nearly everyone agreed: trust is the foundation everything else is built on. Product features, competitive pricing, and brand awareness all matter, but without trust, they struggle to convert buyers.

Source: LinkedIn & Ipsos, 2025 B2B Marketing Benchmark
LinkedIn B2B Marketing Benchmark, 2025

Being "category famous" beats broad brand awareness

When marketers ranked what actually builds trust, 62.3% said being recommended by peers in your industry matters more than having widespread visibility. Being recognized by the right people through voices they already trust is worth twice as much as being a household name.

Source: LinkedIn & Ipsos, 2025 B2B Marketing Benchmark
Edelman-LinkedIn 2025

95% of hidden buyers become receptive

Most buyers research on LinkedIn long before they talk to sales. Strong thought leadership from creators they trust makes them receptive to outreach they'd normally ignore. Even better, 79% of them actively champion new vendors when it's time to make the final decision.

Source: Edelman & LinkedIn, B2B Thought Leadership Impact Report

This is why our approach works: creator campaigns get you in front of buying teams during their research phase, then we convert qualified engagement into booked meetings.

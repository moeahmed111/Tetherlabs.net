Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2025  ·  Last Updated: March 2026

At TetherLabs, we take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights as a visitor or contact.

What Information We Collect

When you contact us or book a strategy call, we collect:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Company name and website if provided
  • Any information you share in your message or intake form

When you visit our website, we may automatically collect:

  • Basic technical data including IP address, browser type, device type, and pages visited
  • Referral source and time spent on pages
  • This data is collected through standard web analytics tools

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and schedule calls
  • Provide and improve our LinkedIn creator marketing services
  • Send relevant updates or follow ups if you have opted in
  • Analyze website performance and improve user experience

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Third Party Services

We use the following third party tools that may process your data:

  • Analytics tools to understand website traffic
  • Scheduling tools to book strategy calls
  • Email tools to manage communication

Each of these services has their own privacy policy governing how they handle your data.

Cookies

Our website may use cookies to improve your browsing experience and analyze traffic. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time. Disabling cookies may affect some functionality of the site.

Data Retention

We retain your personal information only as long as necessary to fulfill the purpose for which it was collected or as required by law. If you request deletion we will remove your data within 30 days.

Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational steps to protect your information from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. However no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Rights

Depending on your location you may have the right to:

  • Access a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information
  • Withdraw consent for communications at any time
  • Lodge a complaint with a data protection authority

To exercise any of these rights email us at info@tetherlabs.net and we will respond within 30 days.

Children's Privacy

Our website and services are not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do we will update the Last Updated date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
